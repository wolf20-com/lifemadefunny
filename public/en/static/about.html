About This Site

What is Life Made Funny?

Life Made Funny is a “relatable moments bank” gathering funny and familiar stories from daily life all around the world. From sports to jobs and relationships, we deliver those “I know that feeling!” moments in multiple languages for you to enjoy and laugh at.
We share small daily happenings and universal experiences you can nod along to, in various languages and from many countries.

Features

  • Multilingual Support: Not just Japanese, but also available in English, Spanish, French, German, and Portuguese.
    Enjoy relatable moments adapted to each country and culture.
  • New Content Daily: We publish new “relatable moments” every day in different categories and themes!
  • Extensive Genre & Tag Search: Find relatable content using tags for your favorite genres.
  • Easy to Read & Share: Our short content is easy to enjoy and share on social media.

How to Use

  • Check out “relatable moments” from the top page or category pages!
  • If you relate, share them on social media and enjoy with friends.
  • Explore your favorite themes using categories and tags.
